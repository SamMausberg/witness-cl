# Witness-CL v5: primary-source audit

Checked 8 September 2026. This is a focused novelty audit, not a systematic review or an independently reproduced leaderboard. Source titles, identifiers, revisions, benchmark availability, and the claims below were checked against original papers and author-maintained resources. “Reported” describes the source authors' evidence; “boundary” is our assessment of what that evidence does not establish.

## What the motivating benchmark actually establishes

In the June CL-Bench paper, full-history ICL with Sonnet 4.6 has the highest aggregate reported reward and gain among the evaluated systems. This does **not** establish that every memory system loses on every comparison: GPT-5.4 ICL and Mem0 report normalized gains of 20.1% and 20.2%, respectively, with overlapping uncertainty, although ICL has higher normalized reward. Full history is retained only while the context window permits it. Consequently, the required control is the same backbone with full-history ICL, its own stateless baseline, and equal total resources. The exact full-history planner in Witness-CL v4 is a different algorithm and cannot stand in for this control. [CL-Bench v1, Sections 4–5](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.05661v1).

The official benchmark subsequently merged a correction for mixed Cohort Studies reward scales on 19 July. Aggregate numbers from the June paper and a later checkout must not be mixed. Pin the scorer, recompute old results consistently where possible, and retain both native reward and normalized gain. [Official scoring correction, PR #9](https://github.com/pgasawa/continual-learning-bench/pull/9).

A later **open submission**, Ouroboros, reports normalized reward 0.2301 and gain 0.1985 from five stateful rollouts plus its stateless baseline. Its authors disclose substantially more inference compute, disabled core evolution, and positive gains in some domains but negative gains in others. It is a useful additional baseline to reproduce, not independently verified evidence of universal superiority or online harness evolution. [Official benchmark PR #10, July 29 submission](https://github.com/pgasawa/continual-learning-bench/pull/10).

## Twelve closest research sources

| Primary source and checked version | Strongest relevant result | Boundary for this project |
|---|---|---|
| [CL-Bench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.05661v1), Asawa et al., June 4, 2026 | Six domains with shared latent structure; stateful–stateless gain distinguishes use of experience from base capability. | Finite observed schedules do not prove lifelong retention. Some schedules drift; stationary reset certificates do not cover an entire drifting schedule. |
| [AgentCL / MemProbe](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.02461v2), Shu et al., June 2, 2026 | Controlled compositional streams, naive streams, a frozen-memory second pass, and held-out tasks separate reuse, stability, and generalization. MemProbe combines interactions, insights, and skills. | Controlled-stream gains can coexist with weak or harmful held-out reuse. A syntactic checker and LLM consolidation judge are not guarantees of downstream policy value. |
| [ACE](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.04618v3), Zhang et al., March 29, 2026 revision | Incremental playbook generation, reflection, and curation reduce loss of useful contextual detail; the paper reports online and offline adaptation gains. | Its own limitations identify reliance on a capable reflector. The same approach performs poorly in CL-Bench's tested configuration; reported gains cannot be transferred across benchmarks without replication. |
| [ALMA](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.07755v1), Xiong, Hu, and Clune, February 8, 2026 | A meta-agent searches executable memory schemas, update rules, and retrieval mechanisms; learned designs improve sequential decision tasks. | The paper's limitations say design search uses a predefined learning set, with online design search left untested because of rollout cost. Predeployment meta-learning remains compatible with a no-offline-retraining-after-deployment contract, but is not free online adaptation. |
| [TTHE](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.08124v1), Nie et al., July 9, 2026 | Persistent executable harness changes are proposed and selected from unlabeled execution evidence. Selection errors and candidate coverage are measured separately. | The main protocol adapts and scores on the same batch. The authors explicitly leave prequential next-batch evaluation, stronger compute matching, and additional seeds unresolved; judge-selected regressions remain possible. |
| [EvoLib](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.14477v2), Xu et al., July 14, 2026 revision | Extracts reusable skills and insights from trajectories, weights their utility and future value, and consolidates them across instances without parameter updates. | Depends on informative self-verification; the authors identify difficult verification, long-horizon mixed streams, distribution shift, and retention as remaining issues. A more elaborate library alone is not our novelty. |
| [Persistent GUI skills](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.04869v1), Hu, Liang, and Zhu, September 4, 2026 | Versioned, iteration-frozen skill libraries and provenance permit longitudinal comparison with an empty-library control across four OSWorld application domains. | Repeated fixed task sets, separate warm-up trajectories, and domain-specific dynamics limit transfer/causal conclusions. Repeated accepted edits sometimes fail to repair the originating task. Frozen snapshots and provenance are already established design choices. |
| [TTT-E2E](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.23675v2), Tandon et al., December 31, 2025 revision | Next-token updates compress context into weights, with initialization meta-trained for test-time learning; reports useful context-length scaling and constant inference latency in its setting. | Long-context language-modeling and retrieval results do not establish protected-skill retention or improved stateful agent reward. Its training recipe is substantial; adapting a generic local model with gradients does not reproduce it. |
| [Deep SPI](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.12312v2), Delgrange, Avalos, and Röpke, January 28, 2026 revision | Connects representation/world-model error and restricted policy updates to safe improvement; supplies an online algorithm and ALE-57 experiments. | Theorems have explicit MDP, policy-neighborhood, support, and regularity conditions. Observed small training loss is not an assumption-free certificate for a partially observed language agent or hidden drift. Learned safe improvement is not a new claim here. |
| [Dynamic shielding](https://arxiv.org/html/2207.13446v1), Waga et al., ATVA 2022 | Learns approximate automata during RL and constructs shields that reduce undesirable exploration in black-box environments. | Online model learning plus an independent admission layer predates Witness-CL. An approximate learned model's consistency does not prove completeness of its environment class; violation reduction is distinct from value retention. |
| [Predictive state representations](https://arxiv.org/abs/1207.4167v1), Singh, James, and Rudary, UAI 2004; arXiv deposit July 2012 | Represents state through predictions of observable action–outcome tests instead of supplied hidden state. | This motivates continuation-based abstraction, but does not make arbitrary language traces finitely representable, identifiable from available probes, or cheap to learn. Do not date the underlying contribution to 2012. |
| [StepMix / EpsMix](https://arxiv.org/html/2306.06265v1), Li et al., ICML 2023 | Conservative exploration with a safe baseline in tabular episodic MDPs, with high-probability episode-wise constraints and regret analysis. | Finite observed states and specified baseline assumptions differ from unknown language-agent abstractions. A bound on policy value also differs from a guarantee that every realized episode avoids a loss. Risk budgets and baseline protection are established antecedents. |

## Reproduction sources and access observations

[CL-Bench's official repository](https://github.com/pgasawa/continual-learning-bench) exposes the runner, task contracts, schedules, scorers, and system implementations. Its README requires Python 3.13 or later and `uv`; Docker is needed for containerized tasks/systems. The [official leaderboard documentation](https://continual-learning-bench.com/leaderboard.html) describes truncated-when-needed ICL and the stateful/stateless comparison. The dynamically loaded table was not available as a complete current ranking in this audit; no current global rank is inferred from cached snippets.

The [AgentCL author page](https://yihengshu.github.io/) links the [official dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/osunlp/AgentCL), including constructed coding and research streams. A separate AgentCL implementation link was not found through the author page and paper in this bounded audit; this is not proof that none exists. The dataset includes evaluation answers and test code; keep unreleased evaluator fields out of learner memory and preserve only the feedback allowed by each native protocol. Dataset-specific source terms are separate from this repository's software license.

Original code was also checked at [Skill-Evo4GUI](https://github.com/LongtaoHu/Skill-Evo4GUI) and [TTT-E2E](https://github.com/test-time-training/e2e). Source availability is not local execution or successful reproduction. The v4 bibliography additionally credits earlier work such as [Voyager](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.16291); persistent executable skills must not be presented as new.

## Novelty judgment

**Established:** online external memory, executable skills, learned memory designs, test-time parameter updates, learned predictive models, conservative exploration, and independent shielding all have close antecedents. The inherited finite mechanism does not resolve their central scale and validity problems.

**Inference:** the promising contribution is a tractable method for selecting experience that changes decisions, with explicit uncertainty and scope, whose benefit survives prequential, resource-matched evaluation. A useful theorem must describe an operational assumption that can be monitored or independently justified, rather than hide the hard learning problem inside a “correct representation” premise.

**Hypothesis:** directing exploration toward *decision regret* rather than counts of distinguishable hidden models may reduce Witness-CL's early reward deficit. This is a candidate experiment, not a novelty claim: it must be compared with optimistic full-history planning and goal-directed exploration, and its checking cost must be counted. The companion [research agenda](RESEARCH_AGENDA.md) states the falsifiers.
