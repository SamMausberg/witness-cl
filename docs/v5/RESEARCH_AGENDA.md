# Witness-CL v5: falsifiable continuation

Prepared 8 September 2026. This document specifies hypotheses and evaluation decisions. It does not claim that the experiments proposed here have run; executed outcomes belong in the versioned result artifacts and claim ledger. The source audit is in [LITERATURE.md](LITERATURE.md).

## Precise target and unavoidable boundary

The target is a deployed system whose state after episode `t` improves its reward on later episodes, without a separate postdeployment offline retraining phase, while preserving declared earlier behaviors within a specified margin and total resource budget. State may include weights, traces, libraries, or code. Online updates between episodes are allowed and charged. “No forgetting” must specify tasks, input distribution, environment version, comparator, probability, and tolerance; freezing parameters or preserving a skill file is insufficient if routing or execution changes.

What remains unsolved is a sufficient **affordable representation of experience** plus a reliable update rule for realistic partial observations, noisy feedback, and drift. The inherited v4 study reports lower mean return than its exact full-history planner despite late-return parity. That is evidence against its current exploration efficiency, not evidence that language-model memory is solved. Its valid finite state bound, deterministic stationary dynamics, public rewards, and true resets remain substantive assumptions.

A universal zero-loss improvement claim is impossible without additional information or slack. Consider two deterministic one-step worlds. The anchor action returns zero in both; the only other action returns +1 in one world and -1 in the other. Histories produced by the anchor are identical. Any learner that improves in the first world must eventually choose the other action with positive probability and therefore incur a negative reward with positive probability in the second. Refusing that action preserves the anchor but cannot improve. The deterministic information-state form is now formalized in Lean; the positive-probability randomized extension here is a written argument, not a newly discovered lower bound. Nonzero risk budgets, informative safe probes, informative prior assumptions, or probabilistic tolerance change the problem in explicit ways.

## H1: measure how probes change decisions

**Hypothesis.** Selecting prepaid probes by worst-case reduction of uncertainty about the best policy will improve prequential reward over v4's model-count information heuristic at the same risk and checking budgets. Model distinctions that never alter the ranking of available policies should contribute no intrinsic exploration reward.

For a fixed finite policy family `P`, objective `g`, and compatible model cover `C`, define

`R_g(C) = min_{p in P} max_{M in C} [max_{q in P} V(M,q,g) - V(M,p,g)]`.

A probe `u` partitions `C` by its *observable* outcomes. Score it by the decrease from `R_g(C)` to the largest residual regret over nonempty outcome cells; combine public goals only with a fixed, declared weighting or worst-goal rule. The model-wise optimal policy in this definition is computed over hypotheses already available to the learner, not from the hidden true world. This is regret restricted to `P`; it is not global Bayes regret, realized cumulative regret, or the unrestricted optimal-control problem.

Retain the same incumbent admission checker and prepaid risk constraint. Freeze programs before checking. Enumerate only within a declared strict cap; an incomplete computation yields UNKNOWN, never permission. Quotient models only when they are behaviorally equivalent for the precise policy/probe/objective/horizon contract being evaluated. Approximate equivalence must not silently replace exact equivalence in certificates.

**Experiment.** Pair the new arm with v4 guided B16, optimistic full-history planning, random probes, and no-probe/no-training ablations. Freeze the policy family, goal aggregation, risk budget, search cap, and tie breaking on development seeds before a fresh held-out run. Report world-table overlap as well as seed separation. Compare mean reward first; also report late return, worst prefix deficit, certificate violations, exact/UNKNOWN rate, evaluated candidates, CPU seconds, peak memory, and evidence growth. Match candidate-check limits and separately impose equal wall-time ceilings. A benefit obtained through additional search is a different result.

**Success threshold.** A positive paired lower 95% interval for mean-reward gain over guided B16, with zero observed certificate violations under the valid simulator contract. This supports the probe heuristic only. Claiming improvement on the stronger baseline requires a separate positive lower bound against full-history planning and a defensible compute comparison.

**Kill criteria.** Drop the heuristic if held-out mean reward does not improve over guided B16, if its apparent gain vanishes under equal checking resources, or if the number of exact states/policies needed makes over 90% of decisions UNKNOWN. Any false certificate under the declared valid contract kills the implementation's soundness claim immediately. A win on this simulator still leaves native language-agent performance unresolved.

**Resources and weak point.** Existing CPU simulator, exhaustive tiny-world oracle, independent checker tests, and run manifests suffice. No model training or accelerator is needed. The main weakness is that exact regret computation may cost more than the exploration it saves; a fixed small policy family may also overlook the useful continuation entirely.

## H2: useful reuse needs observable scope

**Hypothesis.** Reusing typed, provenance-linked rules only inside an observably supported scope will improve later native task reward while reducing errors caused by stale experience. Unknown scope falls back to the backbone's ordinary history-based solver. This is an empirical hypothesis outside the finite v4 contract, not a certificate of the whole benchmark.

Start with native CL-Bench Database Exploration, followed by Blind Spectrum Monitoring and one structurally different domain if the pilot is useful. These priorities are implementation judgments, not claims that the domains are equivalent finite transducers. Pin the official upstream revision, run its unmodified schedules and scorer, and keep unreleased evaluator data outside the agent process. Native post-action feedback may legally reveal a correct answer; preserve it identically across arms, as audited in NATIVE_BENCHMARK.md. The benchmark's generic migration notice must be preserved and must not be replaced by a convenient oracle regime identifier.

A proposed memory entry contains a typed transformation or query template, the observations that support it, and an executable scope test using only permitted observations. For example, reuse of a learned price-unit conversion requires its observed domain and current consistency checks; table-name equality alone does not certify unchanged units. Execute any disambiguating query through the normal tool interface and charge it. Preserve contradictory evidence, invalidate dependent reuse on observed failure, and record the first erroneous action *before* a drift detector responds. A successful detector cannot retroactively protect that action.

**Experiment.** Use the same backbone, tool permissions, prompts, generation settings, time limits, and stream orders for full-history ICL, stateless ICL, ACE, Mem0, and Witness. Add unrestricted reuse, no scope test, no online update, and point-estimate-model ablations. Use one fixed structured-memory control to test whether the checker merely supplies better formatting. Score each episode before updating from its feedback. Separate legal environment feedback from hidden evaluator labels. Include token-matched and total-wall-time-matched views, counting reflection, checking, retrieval, retries, and online gradients.

For retention, run independent shadow evaluations of earlier behaviors before and after later experience, under unchanged environment snapshots and original task conditions. Do not let shadow outcomes train the live agent. Report drift adaptation separately: obsolete behavior should not be preserved simply because it was once good. Use AgentCL's compositional, naive, frozen-memory, and held-out protocols for the next transfer test once its adapter and source data are pinned.

**Success and kill criteria.** Predeclare at least +5 percentage points of native normalized gain over the strongest matched comparator, with a positive lower paired 95% bound, and no more than a two-percentage-point loss on protected old-task success. Report native reward alongside gain so a weak stateless baseline cannot manufacture a favorable headline. Reject the scope hypothesis if stale rules frequently pass guards, if gains disappear at equal resources, or if useful coverage falls below 10%. Any valid no-regression theorem applies only to the explicitly covered subset; report its coverage denominator.

**Resources and weak point.** Begin with a single-domain pilot, then measure actual input/output tokens, cost, environment runtime, and memory growth before budgeting multiple seeds/domains. The official CL-Bench repository supplies native infrastructure; model access is still required for a real ICL comparison. A typed local-model proposal smoke test establishes interface feasibility, not native benchmark superiority. The deepest weakness is observational aliasing: hidden changes can preserve every cheap scope test until after a harmful decision.

## H3: learning proposal order must beat a simple counter

**Hypothesis.** Online training of a proposal ranker increases accepted useful changes per unit total cost compared with count-based, recency-based, and random ordering over the same candidate pool. The learner updates only from past executed traces and permissible checker outcomes; hidden evaluator counterfactuals are excluded.

Keep the admission checker and immutable incumbents fixed while the ranker changes. Compare the trained ranker with a frozen initialization, a count-based scorer, and random order at equal candidate-generation and exact-check budgets. Log proposal quality before selection, acceptance yield, future realized reward, and gradient time separately. If model proposals are introduced, compare matched model-generated candidates under learned and fixed order; otherwise improvements in language-model output can be misattributed to the ranker.

Kill the training claim if count-based ordering matches its held-out reward at lower total cost, if its gain vanishes when candidate sets are identical, or if updated routing degrades protected behaviors. The inherited small neural effect is weak evidence for a major training contribution. Start with a small CPU ranker; introducing trainable backbone weights needs a separate resource estimate and retention test. The TTT-E2E paper is a serious parameter-learning antecedent, but reproducing its pretraining is outside a small inference pilot.

## Statistical and formal discipline

Episodes within a stream are dependent: pair and resample at the independent stream/world level, not at the episode level. Freeze one primary outcome and contrast per confirmatory study; label the rest exploratory or correct their multiplicity. If the upper confidence bound is below a prespecified worthwhile effect, stop for futility. Otherwise an interval crossing zero is inconclusive, not a proven tie. Repeated peeking or adaptive stopping requires a sequentially valid procedure or a fresh held-out run.

Formalization should first cover the executable finite model: truth remains in a filtered cover, exact lower bounds imply admitted improvement, prepaid losses bound prefix debt, and strict caps cannot admit an unchecked proposal. A compiled Lean result certifies those statements as encoded; it does not certify Python/SQL extraction, real reset semantics, hidden-model coverage, or a native agent. The negative two-world example should accompany the positive theorem so the assumptions carry visible meaning. Native abstraction correctness is a separate research obligation.

Profile before accelerating. Share computations across paired policies and model cells only when exact identities justify reuse. CPU batching is the first systems baseline. A GPU implementation is justified only by measured end-to-end bottlenecks, deterministic reduction checks, memory bounds, and agreement with an independent reference; an attractive kernel microbenchmark alone does not establish more learning per unit cost.
