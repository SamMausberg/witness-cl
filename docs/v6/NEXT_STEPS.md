# Remaining problem and a falsifiable next study

The open problem remains a useful, cost-effective system that learns across actual deployed episodes without an offline retraining phase and without losing useful prior behavior. This reference assumes the finite stationary model class, reliable reset, sufficient output alphabet and public reward functions. It does not acquire those assumptions from ordinary language/tool experience. Exact enumeration becomes expensive before those representation questions are solved. Protected reset reward inside that declared class is much narrower than general capability retention or alignment.

The two-bit mechanism is no longer a reason to prefer deeper search: cheap informative actions and the decision-edge diagnostic both solve it, and the three-bit construction defeats depth two. The prior v5 failures remain rejected hypotheses. The v6 held-out results and measured costs determine whether even the narrow gain/debit heuristic deserves further work; a reward gain over the free-information control would also change actual exploration spending and would not isolate planning depth.

## Concrete next hypothesis: reuse exact comparisons in the strongest cheap control

A completed exact comparison over class C remains a sound lower/upper enclosure after genuine feedback shrinks C to C'. This is already an established monotonicity property, not a novel learning theorem. The engineering hypothesis is that disciplined reuse can lower verification overhead and reduce resource-limit fallbacks without changing evidence or checker authority.

Implement a cache tied to immutable candidate and incumbent programs, public reward vector, structural/reset contract, and explicit evidence ancestry. Reuse exact bounds only along a verified shrinking history. Invalidate on class expansion, inconsistent/incomplete evidence, changed programs/objectives, or missing ancestry. Preserve the old lower bound as conservative; do not relabel it as the exact new minimum. If the selector needs a tighter score or admission result, rerun the original checker. Planning explanations and value estimates cannot insert cache certificates.

Compare cached and uncached versions of the same cheap risk-budgeted selector, plus raw-history optimism. Freeze the precise refresh rule before evaluation. First require exhaustive small-model agreement on every reused enclosure and zero protected-value/prefix violations. Then measure all construction, hashing, ancestry, lookup, refresh, update and planning work; include episodes lost to UNKNOWN. Use 8 development streams, followed by 40 newly recorded streams on a predeclared finite family with more persistent ambiguity, and sweep identical resource ceilings. Reuse of a previously studied finite family remains same-family validation.

The tentative success threshold is at least 30% lower total learner time with no lower mean reward and no worse cap-failure rate, plus a positive paired interval for the time saving. Reject the benefit claim if cache overhead removes the saving, conservative stale bounds reduce reward, the win requires silently ignoring validation/ancestry work, or gains vanish against a strong ordinary exact-state memoization control. Any enclosure or ledger failure rejects the implementation. This can run on the current CPU, with bounded per-decision work and no LLM, model-weight update, external agent action or paid compute. Estimate wall time from a short measured pilot; no GPU-hour claim is justified.

## Formal and representation obligations

The next formal result should refine Python-style partial tables to the executable Lean semantics: the denotation of one fitted partial-table frontier must equal exactly the completions that reproduce the new trace. A small-machine differential mismatch or a missing refinement case invalidates that runtime claim. The current 262,144 membership comparisons are evidence, not that general proof.

The larger scientific hypothesis still needs a sufficient learned abstraction in a real resettable environment. A valid experiment must compare fixed-backbone raw-history ICL, strong memory/skill methods and model-based controls at matched total cost, retaining both learning gains and old-task regressions. The current work does not authorize deploying an open-ended learner to obtain that evidence. These are research directions; they are not running, scheduled, or represented as achieved results.
