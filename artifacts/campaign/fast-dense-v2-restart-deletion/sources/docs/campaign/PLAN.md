# Stage 1 registered report: pilot before confirmation

Amended 2026-09-09 following the author's review. GH200 use remains authorized
through the study. This is a Stage 1 manuscript and development protocol, not an
accepted registered report or an established competitive result. Prior source
freezes and failed candidates remain intact.

**Latest operational amendment:** the author subsequently imposed a two-hour
maximum. The 32-stream pilot is paused at 161/17,664 completed episodes. A
separately frozen 240-episode descriptive diagnostic uses two new streams and
smaller probe panels, retaining the full learning sequence and all three arms.
See the [time-budget amendment](TIME_BUDGET_AMENDMENT.md) for the exact assignment,
fixed cutoff, preserved denominator and limits on inference. The original
protocol below remains the uncompleted research target.

The original empirical target is one complete **32-stream pilot**. The four streams
already frozen in `dense-v2/development` are its first block, followed by 28
additional independent streams, with the same model, interface, learner and
generator. The full seed registry is 101100–101131. Inclusion follows the frozen
assignment, not outcomes; no stream is replaced or selected after inspection.
Both blocks together contain 17,664 episodes: 32 streams × 3 arms × 184 episodes.

Each stream has eight initial learning questions, 64 frozen old probes, 16
further learning questions, the same 64 old probes, and 32 final questions.
Every probe uses an isolated checkpoint clone. Full history, official-source
online ACE and delayed memory learn independently. Acquisition, reflection,
checks, failures and every scheduled phase count in full costs. Setup and
offline interventions are reported separately. All assigned outcomes are kept.

The complete pilot report must include:

- Every model-generated admission, changed-binding corroboration and later
  fresh-row use, with the full per-stream/per-arm census even when it is zero.
- Final accuracy and error counts by arm and outer operation, including perfect
  streams, so ceiling effects are visible before selecting confirmation endpoints.
- Paired old-before/old-after correctness on all 64 instances, per-stream
  retention differences, their measured variance and discordant-pair counts.
- Full model token totals, including every update and failed attempt.

Pilot reporting is descriptive. Partial records provide progress counts only.
The provisional research target remains future superiority over each control,
20% fewer total tokens against each, and old-panel noninferiority within two
percentage points. The prior blanket .10 retention SD floor led to roughly 419
confirmation streams; that is a sensitivity scenario, **not an authorized
automatic confirmation schedule**. After the pilot, justify a retention variance
assumption from the observed paired panels and stream variation, assess feasible
power, and freeze a revised confirmatory protocol before any confirmatory calls.
Do not replace independent streams with an instance denominator. Any margin or
endpoint change must be explicit and prospective; the current margin remains
two points. If accuracy is at ceiling, state that limitation and reconsider the
superiority target before confirmation rather than spending an uninformative run.

The mechanism must admit provisionally at t, corroborate the unchanged relation
with changed binding and output in a later correct direct-query episode, and use
it on fresh rows with a new outer computation later still. Promotion never enables
same-episode reuse. Exact provenance, fixed-program interventions and agent reruns
after relation deletion are distinct evidence. A source admission or scripted
capacity check is not an observed model-generated mechanism chain.

Matched extra-query evidence, relation text, immediate admission, stable/drift
and nonreuse controls, plus native CL-Bench contact remain in the research plan.
Their launch is held until the complete pilot census, ceiling and variance report
has been reviewed. A zero census is an interpretable negative, not a reason to
hide the denominator or skip reporting the pilot.

Each study freezes sources/configuration before generation and preserves every
call durably. Recovery continues the identical schedule; unknown usage remains
explicit. The original pilot has no warm-accuracy stop or short wall deadline;
the separate diagnostic has the explicit fixed cutoff recorded above. No confirmation
outcome may tune the learner.

The manuscript has 10–12 pages including references and a page-one claims box.
Keep the finite-dependence counterexample, acquisition break-even inequality and
accurately scoped runtime contracts. Unrelated controller/neural/kernel results
remain in the separately cited technical report. Final claims follow the data.
