# Delayed-corroboration campaign

Authorized 2026-09-09 after repository and paper review. GH200 allocation lasts
until the fixed study completes. This document records intent, not a pre-run
freeze or a positive result. Historical runs and their protocols remain closed.

The five co-primary endpoints are future accuracy superiority to full history
and faithful online ACE; total token ratio below .80 against each; and old-panel
after-minus-before noninferiority above -.02. Each uses a one-sided paired-stream
level .01. All five must pass. A sizing pilot of 32 independent development
streams precedes the final sample-size freeze; independent streams, never rows,
are the replication units. At paired SD .10, the two-point margin requires about
419 streams for .96 endpoint power and at least .80 joint power across five tests.

Each main stream has eight initial learning questions, 64 frozen old probes,
16 further learning questions, the same 64 old probes, and 32 final questions.
Every probe uses an isolated checkpoint clone. All assigned outcomes are kept.
The three main arms learn independently; matched-history causal forks are a
separate experiment. Acquisition, reflection, checks, failures and every scheduled
phase count in full costs. Setup and offline interventions are separately reported.

The mechanism must admit provisionally at t, corroborate an unchanged relation
with changed binding and output at a later correct direct-query episode, and use
that relation on fresh rows with a new outer computation later still. Promotion
never enables same-episode reuse. Whole-run provenance, fixed-program intervention
and memory-deletion reruns must support the trace claim; admission alone does not.

Controls include official-pinned online ACE, identical-derived-SQL text, extra
direct-query evidence, and immediate admission. Run stable/drift paired diagnostics
on 64 fresh streams and nonreuse on 32. Native contact uses the unchanged pinned
CL-Bench DatabaseExploration default questions, five official orderings, ordinary
charged QUERY/ANSWER actions, and stateful/stateless comparisons. Native checks
must precede ANSWER and commit only after ordinary success feedback.

The runner must freeze sources/configuration before generation, preserve every
call durably, checkpoint whole episodes, and resume the identical schedule.
Confirmation has no warm-accuracy early stop. Missing usage or incomplete panels
blocks the full positive headline. No confirmation outcome may tune the learner.

The main paper will have 10–12 pages including references and a page-one box of
three established and three unestablished claims. Keep the finite-dependence
counterexample, acquisition break-even inequality, and accurately scoped runtime
contracts. Move unrelated controller/neural/kernel results into one technical
report cited once. Generate every numerical claim from audited artifacts.
